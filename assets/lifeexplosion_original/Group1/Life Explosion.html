What is Life Explosion

We don't know exactly how to classify this thing. Although it looks and ships as a Windows screen saver, we absolutely don't limit it to the "screen saver" term. Life Explosion may become you favorite toy, and you will spend hours building colourful animations. Almost each pattern you'll arrange will look (at least) nice and interesting. Often your animations will have some psychedelic effect - you will like just to watch them.

How to register

Registration of Life Explosion is FREE! Just click <Get registration code> button and fill required fields (Your name, e-mail) and click <Send me registration code> button. A few minutes later you'll get it by e-mail. Then enter your name and code in registered to form and enjoy.
